# Introducing, [piano.rawsie.dev](https://piano.rawsie.dev/)!!!

Turns out I somewhat make music.

## What is it?

I'm glad you asked! [piano.rawsie.dev](https://piano.rawsie.dev/) is basically a piano midi visualizer that is embedded into the web, and played directly in the browser. No downloads necessary. 

## But why? What's the point?

Well, recently I've started composing music and I wanted a more creative way to display my music than just throwing an MP3 player onto a page. I've also always been fascinated by those midi players you'd see on YouTube. So I just took this as my excuse and finally created my own. 

## What's in it?

Right now, the website features two of my piano pieces. I plan to compose more and update as I go so look forward soon!

If for some reason you're interested in how i got here, read more at [Where the Music Began](../blog/where-the-music-began) blog!

Soon I'll release another post going deep dive into the technicals of this project.
